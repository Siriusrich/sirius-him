About Sirius HIM

Rooted in Runaway Bay. Restless by design.

The story

Sirius HIM comes from Runaway Bay — a stretch of Jamaica's north coast where the sea sits close, the sound systems carry down the road, and music is less a career choice than a way of life. Growing up inside that, you learn early that rhythm is language: a conversation passed between generations, between neighborhoods, between people who have never met.

That foundation is where the sound begins. Dancehall taught pace and confidence — the art of saying everything in few words, on top of a riddim that refuses to sit still. R&B taught patience — melody, breath, the space between notes where feeling lives. Sirius HIM built a style in the gap between the two: melodic, warm, direct, and unmistakably Jamaican.

Independent by choice, the ambition is simple and serious: carry the Runaway Bay sound further than it has ever gone, without sanding down where it came from. International reach, island centre. Every record is a step on that road.
